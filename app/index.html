The map of the route

Every pin is a trophy, numbered in route order and coloured by chapter. Pins mark the district each stop belongs to on this journey — not an exact in-game spot. Click a pin to open its guide.

How the route works

  1. Chapters 1–4 follow the story in order. Chapter trophies unlock on their own — just finish the chapter.
  2. Chapter 5 clears every stranger strand before the finale, while the story is still unfolding.
  3. Chapters 6–8 are free roam: playground, outlaw economy, then loose ends. Do them between story beats or after the finale.
  4. Chapter 9 is the final mission, 100% completion, and the platinum.

MISSABLE flags mark stops where the moment can pass or the attempt can fail — set them up deliberately. Nothing here is permanently locked out on the official descriptions alone.
